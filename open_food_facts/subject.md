Open Food Facts: Hack the global food system
Join us to help hack the global food system and prototype open-source tools used by
millions of people every day, with tailor-made technical challenges: AI, Flutter, JS frontend,
backend, data, or their SDKs across many languages.

◦ Data Imports - structure massive imports of external data (more than half a
million products) and build a modular infrastructure to make this easier.
◦ Official mobile app (Dart/Flutter) - redesign the offline-mode architecture,
two-way synchronization, Open Prices integration, personalized onboarding.
◦ Search-a-licious - Open Food Facts' future search engine: backend fixes,
production deployment, front-end integration with the Explorer, or a Flutter/
Dart mobile port.
◦ Open Food Facts Explorer - build the new reference web interface on the
Webcomponents stack.
◦ Nutri-Patrol & Hunger Games - prototype new gamification mechanics to
speed up product corrections.
◦ Score my recipe (Green-Score) - an environmental scoring tool for recipes
including gamification.
◦ Robotoff (AI) - multiply contributions through Machine Learning.
◦ Open Prices - collaborative collection of prices and inflation data: object
detection to extract barcodes/prices, receipt anonymization, anomaly
detection, dedicated mobile app.
◦ Food API & AI (LLMs) - position Open Food Facts as the source of truth for
AI applications via a community-maintained MCP server.
◦ Rapid Product Acquisition - vision-based (LVLM) data extraction to add a
product from a photo in under a minute.
